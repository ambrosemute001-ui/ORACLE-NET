ABOUT ORACLE NET

A network built around people.

Oracle Net is a community-driven circular waste platform. It connects citizens who have unwanted items with a marketplace and recovery network, while keeping municipal control focused on the streams that actually need intervention.

🧭 Built for a real civic need

UAESP actively seeks technology-enabled solutions for bulky waste, including through its GovTech call Atenea. Oracle Net was built independently in that spirit, designed to plug into Bogotá's real reporting channels, Chatico, the 195 and 110 lines, and existing recycler cooperatives, rather than invent new ones.

📱 Citizen

Report an item, choose free donation or direct sale when appropriate, and follow its journey.

🏛 Municipal Admin

Controls assignments to repairers, recycler cooperatives and authorized final disposal only where intervention is required.

♻️ Recycler Cooperative

Receives assigned recyclable material and confirms collection.

🔧 Repairer

Receives assigned repairable items, restores them and lists them for resale.

Circular Resource Recovery

Collected items are routed by the recovery hierarchy.

Reusable

Good condition → donated locally.

Repairable

Fair-condition items → sent to repairers, fixed, and resold to citizens as affordable second-hand goods.

Recyclable

Metal, wood, appliances → recycler cooperatives.

Residual

Non-recoverable → Doña Juana Sanitary Landfill. Hazardous items go to specialised regulated handling instead, never the ordinary landfill.

About Oracle Net

Oracle Net is a community-driven circular waste platform. It connects citizens who have unwanted items with a marketplace and recovery network, while keeping municipal control focused on the streams that actually need intervention.

🧭 Built as a pilot toward Volumi

Bogotá's Mayor's Office (UAESP) has confirmed that a city initiative called Volumi is already in development - a marketplace guiding residents toward repair, reuse, resale, donation, recycling, and proper disposal for bulky items. Oracle Net is presented as a candidate reference implementation and pilot for that same concept: it follows the same design attributes UAESP has outlined (trusted information, actionable pathways, actor coordination, low-friction access, and support for low-connectivity users), and is built to plug into Bogotá's real reporting channels and operator network rather than replace them.

Collected items are routed by the recovery hierarchy.

Reusable

Good condition → donated locally.

Repairable

Fair-condition items → sent to repairers, fixed, and resold to citizens as affordable second-hand goods.

Recyclable

Metal, wood, appliances → recycler cooperatives.

Residual

Non-recoverable → Doña Juana Sanitary Landfill (final disposal).
